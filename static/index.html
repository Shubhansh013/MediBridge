💊 About MediBridge

MediBridge connects patients to verified independent pharmacies across Delhi NCR to eliminate the frantic physical hunt for medicines during critical moments.

It provides a shared real-time availability network where inventory changes require verified human confirmation before going live.

Implemented Features

  • Live Medicine Search: Search by brand, generic salt, or Hinglish alias.
  • Live Nearby Pharmacies: Haversine distance tracking to verified local shops.
  • Live Salt-Equivalent Substitutes: Direct identification of therapeutic matches.
  • Live Freshness Indicators: Visual staleness badges for records unconfirmed for >24h.
  • Live Natural Language Stock Updates: Chemist chat parsing with 5-stage NLP.
  • Live Audit History & 1-Tap Undo: Append-only log with instant rollback.
  • Planned Patient SOS Broadcasts: Emergency pharmacy alerts (under development).
  • Planned Direct Doctor Consultations: Clinical telemedicine integration.

30-Second Quick-Start Guide

  1. Search Medicine: Tap Search and enter a medicine name (e.g. Dolo 650 or Paracetamol).
  2. Check Status: Review real-time availability badges and salt-equivalent substitutes.
  3. Locate Pharmacy: Switch to Nearby to see pharmacy addresses and approximate distances.
  4. Verify Before Visit: Note the last-updated timestamp and reconfirm with the pharmacy before travelling.
📋 Patient & Chemist Guidelines
🤒 For Patients:
  • Search using brand names, generic formulations, or common colloquial spellings.
  • Review the stock badge (In Stock, Low Stock, or Out of Stock) and the last confirmed time.
  • Always reconfirm with the pharmacy by calling before visiting, as counter sales may deplete stock rapidly.
  • Salt matching does not equal interchangeability: Different brands may differ in release mechanisms, excipients, or dosage forms. Always consult your doctor or licensed pharmacist.
  • Schedule H medicines require a valid doctor's prescription upon physical collection.
👨‍⚕️ For Chemists:
  • Switch to Chemist Mode using the top role switcher and select your active store from the context bar.
  • Note: The top role switcher is a client toggle for demo testing. In the backend, stock proposals strictly require your user account to be linked to the selected pharmacy in the database.
  • Submit stock updates promptly via casual chat messages (e.g., Dolo 650 20 strips available) or template chips.
  • Physical Verification Required: Never confirm a proposed update without inspecting shelf inventory.
  • Adhere strictly to drug scheduling and prescription dispensing regulations.
🛡️ Safety, Verification & Privacy

Prescription & Regulatory Compliance

Schedule H medicines are prominently flagged with Schedule H Compliant badges. These items cannot be dispensed over the counter and require a licensed doctor's prescription. MediBridge never provides dosage instructions or clinical prescribing advice.

Chemist Authorization & Scoping

Chemist updates follow strict database scoping. If a user attempts to propose or confirm stock for a pharmacy they are not linked to in user_pharmacies, the backend immediately rejects the request with an authorization error (HTTP 403 Forbidden).

Supported Stock Statuses

  • 🟢 In Stock: Verified units available on pharmacy shelves.
  • 🟡 Low Stock: Limited inventory remaining; contacting ahead is recommended.
  • 🔴 Out of Stock: Depleted inventory; check salt-equivalent alternatives.
  • ⏱️ Stale (>24h): Unconfirmed for over 24 hours. Inventory data may not reflect recent counter sales.

Audit Trail & Privacy Protection

Every applied change is preserved in an append-only stock_history audit ledger, tracking previous vs. new values and author IDs. Patient searches do not require account registration or health data tracking.

⚖️ Do's, Don'ts & Reporting

Do:

  • Do call the pharmacy to reserve medicines before travelling for urgent needs.
  • Do check the last-verified timestamp on stock records.
  • Do consult a qualified doctor or pharmacist before switching to equivalent salts.
  • Do keep pharmacy inventory updated promptly when stock batches arrive or sell out.

Don't:

  • Don't assume displayed availability is an absolute legal guarantee of shelf stock.
  • Don't treat MediBridge as a replacement for a medical prescription or emergency care.
  • Don't confirm stock updates without physically checking actual shelf inventory.
  • Don't attempt to submit updates for pharmacies you are not authorized to manage.
  • Don't assume matching active ingredients are automatically interchangeable without pharmacist guidance.
⚠️ Reporting Inaccurate Information:
An automated in-app reporting button is currently under development. To avoid generating unmonitored tickets, no placeholder button is shown. If you encounter incorrect stock or pharmacy data, please notify the pharmacy directly or alert pilot administrators.
❓ FAQs, Support & Information

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: Why are no medicines or pharmacies appearing?
    A: MediBridge is currently running a pilot focused on Delhi NCR (Rohini & Saket). Ensure your search matches catalogued items or clear filters.
  • Q: What does a stale stock badge mean?
    A: The pharmacy has not confirmed availability in over 24 hours. Always contact the pharmacy directly to verify.
  • Q: What should I do if a pharmacy's displayed stock is wrong?
    A: Inform the pharmacy staff so they can submit a quick one-tap correction from their Chemist portal.
  • Q: Can MediBridge prescribe medicines or recommend dosage?
    A: Never. MediBridge is strictly an availability discovery platform. It never provides medical advice, diagnosis, or prescribing.
  • Q: What happens if a chemist confirms an update by mistake?
    A: The chemist can navigate to the History tab and tap Undo to instantly revert to the previous stock state.
  • Q: What should I do if a feature is unavailable?
    A: Features such as Patient SOS broadcasts and automated reporting are under active development and will roll out in upcoming pilot phases.
Medical & Availability Disclaimer:
MediBridge helps users discover medicine availability. It does not replace professional medical advice, prescribing, or emergency care. Stock information may change and should be confirmed with the pharmacy.
